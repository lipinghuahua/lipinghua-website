Hi! I’ll be presenting my recent design research project Find Tanger in My Bag at De Fabriek Eindhoven during Dutch Design Week 2026, October 17–25.

The project started during my residency in Tangier, where I got obsessed with a plastic woven bag, people there call it quffa (قُفَّة).
