Hi! I’ll be presenting my recent design research project Find Tanger in My Bag at De Fabriek Eindhoven during Dutch Design Week 2026, October 17–25.

The project started during my residency in Tangier, where I got obsessed with a plastic woven bag, people there call it quffa (قُفَّة).

Liping Hua is a multidisciplinary designer based 80% in Eindhoven (NL) and 20% in Shanghai (CN).
She is obsessed with finding lies, glitches, misalignments but also romance within modern systems of production through everyday life. Working across design research, graphic design and education, she isolates and recomposes observations into visual narratives and mediators of social connection, often taking the form of installations, publications and interactive spaces.
